Constraints

What the book is not allowed to do.

A construction policy is defined as much by its prohibitions as its targets. These bind regardless of how attractive something looks in the moment.

No leverage in the flagship

Borrowed money turns a drawdown you can survive into one you cannot. The flagship mandate runs unlevered.

No unlisted venue balances

Overnight balances sit only on venues from a written whitelist. The list is not revised because a trade would be convenient.

No position without a thesis

If the case and its falsification criteria are not in writing, the position does not exist — including for assets we already like.

Confirm these match your written risk document before publishing.
