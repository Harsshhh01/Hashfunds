Process

How a position gets into the book.

STEP 01

Written thesis

The case and the counter-case both go in writing before any capital moves. If it cannot be argued on paper, it does not get bought.

STEP 02

Falsification criteria

The thesis must name the specific, observable events that would make it wrong — written down before the position exists, not decided afterwards.

STEP 03

Sizing & limits

Size is set by worst-plausible-day cost to the fund, not by conviction. Conviction decides whether we hold it at all.

STEP 04

Standing review

Every thesis is re-tested monthly against its own falsification criteria. Breaches close the position without a vote.

Risk framework

Limits that bind the people who wrote them.

Risk limits are written down and enforced mechanically, not renegotiated in the moment. When a threshold trips, de-risking begins — the review happens afterwards, because deliberation is slowest at exactly the point speed matters most.
