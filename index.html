The firm

We built the risk framework first, and the product second.

HashFunds was founded in March 2024. The first months went into writing the risk limits, the thesis template and the custody policy — before taking on anyone else's capital, because a framework written after the fact tends to be written to justify whatever already happened.

We are deliberately small and founder-led. That is a genuine trade-off: you give up the comfort of a large institution's balance sheet, and you get a manager who has personally read every contract, sized every position, and will answer the phone when something goes wrong.

Process

How a position gets into the book.

We target an annual return in the 13–40% range across our mandates — a target, not a promise, and the spread is wide because digital asset markets are. We are a young firm, so we will not wave a track record at you. What we can show you is the process meant to produce one, and you can judge whether it is the discipline you want handling your capital.

STEP 01

Written thesis

Nothing is bought on a feeling. The case, the counter-case and the specific events that would prove it wrong all go in writing first.

STEP 02

Falsification criteria

Every thesis names the conditions under which it is wrong, before the position exists. Deciding that afterwards is just rationalising.

STEP 03

Sizing by downside

Size is set by what the position can cost on its worst plausible day. Conviction decides whether we hold it at all — never how large.

STEP 04

Standing review

Each thesis is re-tested monthly against its own criteria. If it has broken, the position closes regardless of where the price is.

Target allocation · Model

Sized by drawdown tolerance, not by market cap.

Weighting a book by market capitalisation is an assumption that the market has already done your work. We size each position against how much of the fund it can reasonably cost us on its worst plausible day, then let conviction fill the rest.

A target construction model, not a statement of current holdings. Actual weights vary with the opportunity set and each client's mandate.

In development

Tokenisation, built rather than talked about.

We are building a settlement layer for tokenised real-world assets, with our own digital asset and a fully reserved stablecoin as its cash leg. Tokenisation mostly fails today at the boring end — settlement, custody and redemption — so that is the end we are starting from.

Nothing here has launched. No token or stablecoin is being offered, sold or distributed by HashFunds. There is no sale, presale, whitelist or allocation, and no launch date is being committed to. This section describes work in progress and nothing on it is an offer, an invitation to invest, or a promise that any of it will ship. Issuing a token or a stablecoin is a regulated activity in most jurisdictions, and we will not put anything live before the legal and audit stages above are complete.
