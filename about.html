HashFunds was founded in March 2024. We are not going to pretend to a decade of history we do not have — what we will do is show you exactly how decisions get made here, and let you judge the process rather than a marketing number.

Origin

The framework came before the capital.

Most firms in this asset class write their risk policy after something has already gone wrong, which means it gets written to justify what happened. We did it the other way round: the limits, the thesis template and the custody policy existed before there was any outside money to apply them to.

That is the honest pitch for a firm founded in 2024. We cannot show you how we behaved in 2022, because we were not there. What we can show you is a written process you are free to interrogate line by line — which venues we will hold a balance on overnight, what closes a position, and who signs off. Ask for it and you will get the actual document, not a summary.

The firm is run by an engineer rather than a career allocator, and that shapes it. A B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science is not a finance qualification, and we are not going to dress it up as one. What it does give you is someone who builds the measurement before the position, treats a model as a hypothesis rather than an oracle, and can read the smart contract instead of taking someone's word for what it does.

Being new cuts both ways, and you should weigh both. There is no long record to reassure you. There is also no legacy book, no inherited positions and no incentive to defend a decision made by someone who has since left.

Every position the firm holds carries a written thesis with explicit falsification criteria. If the thesis breaks, the position closes — regardless of price.

In development

Where the firm is heading.

Alongside managing capital, we are building settlement infrastructure for tokenised real-world assets — our own digital asset, and a fully reserved stablecoin to act as its cash leg.

The interesting problem in tokenisation is not issuing a token, which anyone can do in an afternoon. It is settlement, custody and redemption holding up when someone wants their money back at the worst possible moment. That is an engineering problem before it is a finance one, which is why this firm is attempting it.

See the development stages
Nothing here has launched. No token or stablecoin is being offered, sold or distributed by HashFunds. There is no sale, presale, whitelist or allocation, and no launch date is being committed to. This describes work in progress — it is not an offer, an invitation to invest, or a promise that any of it will ship.

Issuing a token or a stablecoin is a regulated activity in most jurisdictions, and the rules are still moving. Independent legal review and a published third-party audit both sit ahead of anything going live.
